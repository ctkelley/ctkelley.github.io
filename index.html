La commedia è finita!

My NC State career (1978-2025) is over.
I no longer have an NCSU office, phone number, or surface mail address. Email to ctk@ncsu.edu still works.

NC State, it's been a pleasure doing business with you. Thank you very much and goodbye.


My post-retirement professional activities are
  • publishing papers on non-smooth optimization, linear/nonlinear equations, mixed-precision algorithms, and particle transport problems,
  • working on the DOE funded CARRE project, a PSAAP IV center,
  • one more book,
  • the SIAM Advancement Advisory Committee , Financial Management Committee , and Book Committee

New stuff

I'm part of the new DOE Center for Advancing the Radiation Resilience of Electronics (CARRE) , a PSSAP IV center.
I will be working (very) remotely as a professor, senior research, in the School of Nuclear Science and Engineering at Oregon State University.

My book: Solving Nonlinear Equations with Iterative Methods: Solvers and Examples in Julia published in print on Oct 31, 2022, just in time for holiday giving.
Check out the Julia package and the Notebook .
